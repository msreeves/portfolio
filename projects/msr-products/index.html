MSR Products

Inquiry-first demonstration store (Fieldcrest Supply Co.) on a Vite-built WooCommerce theme — catalogue UX, SKU-matched imagery, and a portfolio companion app.

What we optimised for

  • Live shop — browse products, galleries, compare, and enquiry flows on the public URL.
  • SKU-accurate imagery — thumbnails tied to catalogue SKUs so bags, bottles, and gear read correctly.
  • Product Companion — React browse demo on this portfolio (search, categories, inquiry CTAs).
  • Design frames — Figma-style board below; each frame is the real storefront at 1440×900.
