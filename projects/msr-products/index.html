MSR Products

Inquiry-first shop catalogue on a Vite-built WooCommerce theme. Design screens live in Figma as the source of truth; product photography is matched to Magento Luma SKUs so bags, bottles, and gear read correctly in the UI.

What we optimised for

  • Design-first workflow — local theme captured into Figma; portfolio links the design file, not a generic mock.
  • SKU-accurate imagery — thumbnails derived from each product SKU (e.g. Wayfarer Messenger 24-MB05), not random stock.
  • Companion browse — React catalogue demo for portfolio visitors (search, category chips, inquiry CTAs).
  • Catalog mode — cart/checkout quiet; contact and inquiry routes carry the commercial story.
