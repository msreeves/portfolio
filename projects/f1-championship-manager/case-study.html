F1 Championship Manager

A personal tool that bridges PS2 TV spectator mode on PAL Formula One titles with a persistent, interactive championship — seasons, points, and standings the games never track on their own.

My role: product concept, Python/FastAPI dashboard, SQLite data model, and PCSX2-adjacent workflow (manual lock-in on race pages; no cloud accounts).

The idea

Studio Liverpool’s PAL Formula One games on PS2 (2001–2006) are brilliant for spectator mode: you pick a race, watch the AI field, and get the drama of a Grand Prix on the couch. What they do not give you is a serious season layer — cumulative points, your own championship narrative, careful verification before results stick, or a single place to move from “I watched Melbourne” to “here is the table after round three.”

F1 Championship Manager is that bridge: the emulator and game stay the spectacle; the dashboard is the championship brain. You still play or spectate on the PS2; you stage classifications on the web UI, preview points, and approve only when you are happy — same discipline I use on labelled programme demos elsewhere on this portfolio.

What it closes

  • Spectator-only gap — TV mode ends when the credits roll; the tool keeps season context, calendars, and standings across rounds.
  • Trust gap — staged results and preview-before-approve so a mis-typed position does not corrupt the championship database.
  • History gap — official vs reversed grids, non-starters flagged, and 2001–2006 season pickers aligned to PAL discs (PCSX2).
  • Ops gap — backups before approved writes, reliability page, and autostart so the dashboard is ready when you sit down to race.

Implementation

  • Python 3.12+, FastAPI, Jinja templates, SQLite (approved vs staged states).
  • Workspace entry: npm run f1cm:launch · open the dashboard demo from the programme card.
  • Historical calendars/grids imported into staging; human verification before grids promote to official tables.
  • Optional PINE/diagnostic hooks for PCSX2 identity — read-only phase; race capture stays manual and honest.

Verification

This is a personal hobby championship rig, not a commercial product. pytest guards the data layer; browser checks are optional. Use Open demo on the programme card when the dashboard is running.
